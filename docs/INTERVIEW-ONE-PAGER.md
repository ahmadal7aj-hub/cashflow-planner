# Interview one-pager (print this)

**Goal:** learn how people handle money between paydays. Listen more than you talk. Never pitch.
**Time:** 30-40 min. **Ids only:** write `P01`, `P02`... never names.

## 1. Consent (read aloud, get a yes)

"This is research about managing money, not a sales call. I will take notes and will not ask for real
account numbers. You can skip anything or stop at any time. Notes are stored under a code, not your name."

## 2. Warm-up (3 min)

"Tell me a bit about your month: when you are paid, and the big payments that go out."

## 3. Questions (15-20 min, product not mentioned)

1. Last time you were unsure if you could afford something before payday: walk me through it.
2. How do you decide today whether you can spend? What do you check?
3. Which apps, spreadsheets or notes do you use? Likes and dislikes?
4. Which payment has surprised you? How often?
5. When in the month is it tightest? How do you cope?
6. Ever paid for a finance tool? Why or why not? What felt fair?
7. Would you enter your salary and bills by hand? What would make it worth it? Would importing a
   statement matter more?
8. How would you feel about your financial data being stored in the cloud, versus only on your phone?
   What would make you trust an app with it?
9. Do you send money home or hold money in other currencies? Would you want Arabic?

**Probes:** "Say more." "What happened next?" "How did that feel?" "Can you give an example?"
**Avoid:** "Would you use...?", "Do you like...?", anything that suggests the answer.

## 4. Prototype (10-15 min) - see the walkthrough script

Ask them to think aloud. Do not coach. Note where they hesitate.

## 5. Wrap-up (3 min)

"What would make you use something like this every week?" and "What is missing?"

## Capture after the session (private notes only)

Pain moments in their words - tools used - surprises - manual-entry attitude (H3) - month vs payday
thinking (H4) - price expectation (H5) - import interest - top 3 takeaways.
